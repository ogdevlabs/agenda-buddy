All 15 ACs attested — 14 with a named test plus live curl/`aspire` CLI evidence against a real AppHost
(all 8 processes: 7 services + `Gateway`), 1 (AC9/AC10's timeout/expiry-specific mechanics) relying on
T09's unit suite alone, as the task brief itself anticipated might be necessary. Full write-up, AC table,
and evidence in
[`docs/pdlc/design/api-gateway-and-mobile-contract/verification.md`](../../design/api-gateway-and-mobile-contract/verification.md).

**Test counts, re-run in full at this gate (not taken on faith from prior tasks' closing notes):** backend
468 (`agenda-buddy-backend.slnf`), integration 230 (`AgendaBuddy.IntegrationTests`, real MongoDB
Testcontainer via Rancher Desktop), mobile 165 — 158 passed, 7 skipped (`MobileApp.Tests`,
`/p:MobileWorkloads=false`). Total **863**, 0 failing.

**One defect found live, invisible to all 863 automated tests:** the Gateway's route allowlist
(`Gateway/AspireServiceDiscoveryProxyConfigProvider.cs`) has no entry for `api/v1/messages/**` or
`api/v1/notifications/**` — both real, correct routes on the Customer service, unreachable through the one
address `MobileApp` is configured to call. `MobileClientRouteResolutionTest` (F-015-T07) didn't catch it
because it fires requests at the hosted domain services directly, bypassing the Gateway. Not fixed here — a
verification task doesn't implement fixes — filed as a follow-up; full reproduction and root cause in
`verification.md` §3.1.
