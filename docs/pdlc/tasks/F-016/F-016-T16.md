Security AC for threat T-003 (HIGH). Implements ADR-026. SCOPE ADDITION beyond the approved PRD, authorised by the maintainer at the Step 12 design gate.
Authenticating this route is nearly worthless on its own: POST /api/v1/auth/register is anonymous, unverified and unrate-limited, so an attacker self-registers as a Customer, obtains a valid token, and pages through the ENTIRE customer table exactly as before - and totalCount even tells them how many pages to fetch. PAGINATION BOUNDS THE RESPONSE, NOT THE EXTRACTION.
Atlas reframed it as a product question rather than a control question: who is this endpoint FOR? F-003 defines discovery as customers finding PROVIDERS, not each other. No flow lists every customer; the only defensible caller is a provider. So: AssertRole(user, "Provider") on the LIST route.
ONLY THE LIST. GET /api/v1/customers/{email} stays authenticated-but-not-role-gated, because a customer legitimately reads their own record through it. DEFERRED, NOT REJECTED: scoping results to the calling provider's own SubscribedCustomerCollection is the stronger fix and was weighed at the gate - it is a real behaviour change and more work; the role check blocks the actual attack path now.
