EstimatedRevenue is REMOVED, not corrected (requirement 18, ADR D-7). The formula was completed × the sum of ALL active service fees — a provider with services at 50 and 80 and two completed appointments was reported as earning 260 — and it cannot be fixed by arithmetic because AppointmentEntity records no service, no fee and no amount. The input does not exist. revenueAvailable:false plus a reason, because a plausible number would be believed and a dashboard reading zero looks like a fact rather than a bug.
Blast radius swept first: ProviderReport and EstimatedRevenue appear NOWHERE outside Library and Library.Tests. Zero production consumers, so the shape change is free today and expensive after F-015. One existing test asserted 50m and is REPLACED, not silently dropped — F-014's one deleted pre-existing test, same class as F-016's ADR-025 and F-021's ADR-034.
Threat T-207: a provider deactivates THEMSELVES. Role plus ownership, and no administrative bypass because there is no administrative role (ADR-025).
FOUND BY DISPATCHING IT: DeactivateProviderCommandHandler called mediator.Publish(request) — publishing the COMMAND, an IRequest<string> and not an INotification. It compiles (Publish has an object overload) and throws at runtime. The handler could never have completed, and nobody knew because nothing had ever dispatched it. DeactivateProviderEvent existed for exactly this and had zero references. Also converted its IsActive write to a targeted $set.
