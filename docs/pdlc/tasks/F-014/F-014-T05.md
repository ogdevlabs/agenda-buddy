Threat T-205 has three holes and only two are closable. (a) Participant emails come from the STORED APPOINTMENT, never the body — PaymentRequest has no such fields. (b) A second charge for the same appointment answers 409. (c) The AMOUNT is client-asserted and CANNOT BE VALIDATED, because an appointment does not record which service it was booked for — the same gap that makes revenue uncomputable. A customer can pay 0.01 for a 50 session.
(c) is accepted and documented in three places rather than quietly left: the threat model, api-contracts §2, and PaymentRequest's own doc comment. It matters little while the gateway records rather than charges; it matters a great deal to whoever first configures a real key.
